Dear Students

For your final project in this course, you are tasked with designing and implementing an AI-driven detection model tailored to a specific threat profile. Your project deliverables must demonstrate how specific machine learning and deep learning models and techniques can be applied to log data to effectively classify and detect a cyber attack.

Your first objective is to select a primary attack category and its associated techniques that can be effectively detected using either network or host logs. You must ensure that there are viable public datasets available for your chosen target.

Note: you are not allowed to select DDoS as an attack technique as it was the topic of Home Assignment #2

Note: We highly recommend aligning your project with the MITRE ATT&CK framework. However, please be aware that not all MITRE techniques and sub-techniques are visibly reflected or detectable within publicly available log telemetry.

In parallel to exploring your target attack techniques, you need to identify relevant, publicly available log data—such as system event logs, network traffic metadata, or application-level traces—that contains the necessary security telemetry to detect the malicious behaviour.

Deliverables for Next Week

You must submit a preliminary document containing the information listed below. We will review these submissions together in class next week to ensure your project scope is on the right track:

Group Members: List all participating students in your team.
Chosen Attack Technique: Identify the specific attack and its corresponding MITRE ATT&CK ID (if applicable).
Attack Characteristics & Log Mapping: Define the main characteristics of the attack and explicitly explain how these behaviours are reflected in network or host logs.
Target Datasets: Identify two publicly available datasets that you will use for training and testing your ML/DL models.
Academic Literature Validation: Provide citations for two peer-reviewed academic papers that have successfully implemented ML and/or DL techniques to detect this specific attack technique.
